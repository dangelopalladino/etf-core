## ❌ Forbidden Words & Phrases (User-Facing)

## Clinical / Diagnostic Language

* Diagnostic / diagnosis / diagnose
* Clinical / clinically
* Psychological evaluation / psychological assessment
* Psychological testing
* Mental health screening
* Mental health assessment
* Psychometric *(internal use only)*
* Validated instrument / clinically validated *(not yet true)*
* Evidence-based treatment

## Treatment Language

* Treatment / treat
* Therapy / therapeutic / therapist
* Intervention
* Prescription / prescribe
* Prognosis
* Symptom / symptoms
* Disorder
* Condition
* Mental illness / mental health condition

## Identity/Labeling Language

* "You are [type]" — e.g., "You are a Compass"
* "You have [anything]" — e.g., "You have low identity clarity"
* "You suffer from..."
* "You struggle with..."
* "Your personality reveals..."
* "Your assessment shows you need..."
* "You should seek help for..."

## Credentialing Overreach

* Licensed therapist / licensed psychologist *(you are neither)*
* Board certified
* Clinically trained
* "Validated" — until peer-reviewed study is published
* "Proven" — unless you have published data
* "Scientifically proven"
* "240 unique results" *(personalization marketing claim — see Section 8)*
